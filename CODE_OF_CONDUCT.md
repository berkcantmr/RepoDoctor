# Code of Conduct

RepoDoctor aims to provide a respectful and constructive environment for everyone.

Participants must communicate professionally, accept technical disagreement without personal attacks, and avoid harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or disclosure of another person's private information.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and communication that violate these expectations. Serious or repeated violations may result in a temporary or permanent ban from project spaces.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through the contact method listed on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed in good faith and handled as confidentially as practical.
